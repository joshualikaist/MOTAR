## Two distinct novelty questions

**Evidence-aware robust arbitration itself:** **NOVELTY_NOT_VERIFIED**. Shared-model baseline regret, uncertainty-aware fallback, belief-state decisions and safety mediation have direct predecessors. The proposed hypothesis ledger/utility refusal may be a useful implementation, but combining these principles does not establish originality. No new safe-improvement, stability, recursive-feasibility or coverage theorem is claimed. A short-horizon empirical score does not inherit a theorem for a different uncertain MDP.

**The occlusion/reacquisition/capture/backup problem and decision:** **CANDIDATE_NOVEL only as a precisely specified method/application question**, not “first” unified tracking/capture. FASTER does not establish target sensing; BPOD does not establish uncertain raw-scene geometry; active tracking methods do not by themselves establish calibrated intervention over Frozen F. Whether the concrete proposed opportunity window and ambiguity handling are distinguishable and useful requires broader prior-art reading and the mechanism-controlled tests below. A POMDP can already represent terminal rewards and sensor ambiguity; the problem's vocabulary is not a new formalism.

## At most three possible paper contributions

1. **Evidence-supported intervention timing:** define and validate the first closing window of beneficial, evidence-supported, next-cycle-backup-feasible action options while a target is still observed. Falsifier: it does not improve collision-free capture or intervention efficiency versus reactive/immediate variants with the same safety and evidence models. Viability, active perception and event-triggered control overlap still require comparison. **CANDIDATE_NOVEL**, not a theorem.
2. **Ambiguity-selective mission arbitration:** construct a measured-evidence hypothesis set that separates collision preservation from target-utility inference, and calibrate the paired action-option benefit/refusal against actual one-step-then-F outcomes. Falsifier: ambiguity-aware refusal has no advantage over plug-in prediction or standard robust baseline regret at matched action/sensor/safety budgets. Robust regret and abstention are reused principles. **NOVELTY_NOT_VERIFIED** until a specific estimator/algorithm and matched evidence exist.
3. **Reproducible mission and failure evaluation:** distinguish coverage failure, false association, no certified backup and actual capture/crash/timeout, using independent seeds and qualified common-prefix counterfactuals. Falsifier: no practical result or no stable conclusions across lineages/conditions. This is an empirical/methodological candidate, **not a new statistical estimator or stand-alone navigation theorem**.

No contribution may be claimed for radius inflation alone, whole-body geometry alone, adding next-cycle stopping, copying an F candidate, generic Gaussian prediction, Monte Carlo visibility, threshold/hysteresis tuning, a weighted objective, or assembling known components. C02 CPU corrections are necessary validity work. Domestic-conference suitability is not established by this table; absent a distinct mechanism and mission evidence, the method claim may remain NOT_NOVEL_ENOUGH.
