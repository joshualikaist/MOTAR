## Why

CAM-S2a (from scratch, 3.0 rad/s yaw scale) is the camera-only policy bound for the real vehicle, whose yaw rate is
limited to 60 deg/s (1.0472 rad/s, PX4 `MPC_YAWRAUTO_MAX`). CAM-S2a-E measures that policy with its yaw command clipped
at 1.047 rad/s (arm YC12k; at 205 bars / seed 4501 its identity cell crashed in 51.7 % of the quota
episodes against 35.4 % unclipped,
`~/motar_tmp/cams2a_eval_20261124/raw_a1/identity_01/identity_report.json`; context only, not a result of this unit).
S2b-FT continues CAM-S2a ep12000 for about 3,000 epochs with `NAVRL_YAW_RATE_MAX = 1.0472` (density 175 → 205), so the
policy learns the real ceiling instead of being clipped. The first flights may use 45 deg/s; the training preregistration
defers to this evaluation whether a 0.785 rad/s clip of S2b-FT "loses materially" (which would propose a dedicated
45 deg/s training run).

## Questions

1. **G1 (flight candidate).** In simulation, is S2b-FT in its own training environment (yaw scale 1.0472) better on crash
   and capture than CAM-S2a ep12000 clipped at 1.047 rad/s in CAM-S2a's environment (A60, identical to CAM-S2a-E's YC12k)?
2. **G2 (45 deg/s).** Does S2b-FT tolerate its yaw command clipped at 0.7854 rad/s (FT45; action scale 1.0472 unchanged)?
   CAM-S2a-E's gate-Y rule, including the minimum binding share.
3. **Reported, not gated:** capture kinematics, crash split (target contact vs obstacle and the other causes), blind
   behaviour, the yaw binding shares, per-density rates.

## Analysis (fixed before the run)

Blocks are (density, seed): 175, 205 × 4501–4503 = 6 blocks; rates are per-block quota rates (`gr_analyse.rates`,
1,024 episodes per cell). Intervals as CAM-S2a-E with this grid's degrees of freedom (`cams2a_eval_analyse`, imported
unchanged): a stratified percentile bootstrap (10,000 resamples, seed 20,261,124, seeds resampled within density) and a
stratified t-interval with df = Σ(n_d − 1) = **4** (t₀.₉₇₅ = 2.7764). Contrasts in pp; a label needs both intervals to
agree. Crash labels: CAM-K2-E's gate-A rule with CAM-K2-E3's names (MATERIAL_REDUCTION both upper ends < −5; SMALL_REDUCTION
both upper ends < 0 and mean ≤ −2; WORSE both lower ends > 0 and mean ≥ +2; NEGLIGIBLE both inside (−2, +2); UNRESOLVED
when the intervals disagree on the side; NO_DETECTED_CHANGE otherwise; a reduction with timeouts up ≥ 2 pp reads
CRASH_TO_TIMEOUT). Capture and timeout labels: C2's scheme, bands 3 pp and 2 pp (LOWER / SMALL_LOWER / HIGHER /
SMALL_HIGHER / NEGLIGIBLE / NO_DETECTED_CHANGE / UNRESOLVED).

| Gate | Contrast | Verdict rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **G1** | FT − A60 | better = crash ∈ {SMALL_REDUCTION, MATERIAL_REDUCTION, CRASH_TO_TIMEOUT} or capture HIGHER; worse = crash WORSE or capture LOWER. better and worse → **FT_TRADEOFF**; worse → **FT_WORSE**; better with crash ∈ {NEGLIGIBLE, SMALL_REDUCTION, MATERIAL_REDUCTION, CRASH_TO_TIMEOUT} and capture ∈ {NEGLIGIBLE, SMALL_HIGHER, HIGHER} → **FT_BETTER** (a capture gain never hides a crash rise, not even one below the 2 pp floor carried by NO_DETECTED_CHANGE; review r1 P2-3); crash NEGLIGIBLE and capture ∈ {NEGLIGIBLE, SMALL_HIGHER} → **FT_NO_MATERIAL_DIFFERENCE**; otherwise **FT_UNRESOLVED**. Without bound A60 cells: **G1_NOT_ANSWERED** (a60_source A60_NOT_REPRODUCED or A60_PATH_FAIL, or RERUN_REQUIRED without stage a60) or **A60_BINDING_FAIL** (an A60 binding below fails). a60_source A60_NOT_RUN also reads G1_NOT_ANSWERED. |
| **G2** | FT45 − FT | `[yawclip]` telemetry missing in any FT45 cell → **YAWCLIP45_INACTIVE**; pooled binding share of the FT45 cells (requested \|yaw\| above 0.7854, recorder rows, exact) < 1 % → **YAWCLIP45_WEAKLY_BINDING**; crash WORSE or capture LOWER → **YAWCLIP45_SENSITIVE** (= "loses materially" in the training preregistration's sense); crash ∈ {NEGLIGIBLE, SMALL_REDUCTION, MATERIAL_REDUCTION} and capture ∈ {NEGLIGIBLE, SMALL_HIGHER, HIGHER, SMALL_LOWER} → **YAWCLIP45_TOLERATED** (admits a capture loss whose intervals exclude 0 but whose mean is under 3 pp); otherwise **YAWCLIP45_UNRESOLVED**. The binding shares of FT (same reference) and A60 (its own) are reported next to it. |

How the labels are read (the decisions remain the user's): FT_BETTER → in simulation, training at the ceiling beats
clipping CAM-S2a and S2b-FT is the 60 deg/s flight candidate; FT_NO_MATERIAL_DIFFERENCE → no difference beyond the bands,
either policy, S2b-FT matching the vehicle natively; FT_WORSE or FT_TRADEOFF → keep CAM-S2a with the clip as the 60 deg/s
candidate, or decide on the trade; FT_UNRESOLVED → no recommendation from G1. YAWCLIP45_TOLERATED → a 45 deg/s first
flight may clip S2b-FT; YAWCLIP45_SENSITIVE → a dedicated 45 deg/s training run is proposed (the training
preregistration's rule); UNRESOLVED / WEAKLY_BINDING / INACTIVE → no recommendation. FT_BETTER reached through
CRASH_TO_TIMEOUT is reported with that crash label (crashes became timeouts, capture unchanged within the band).

## Disclosed limits

- The two arms of G1 differ in more than the yaw handling: FT has trained ~3,000 more epochs, up to 205 bars, with a
  lower learning rate and the ceiling in its observation normaliser (`robot_body_angvel / yaw_rate_max`) and its
  previous-action channel; A60 never trained above 175 bars. G1 compares the two deployable options, not the cause.
- A60 clips at 1.047 rad/s (CAM-S2a-E's value, string "1.047"); FT's ceiling is 1.0472 rad/s (0.0002 rad/s apart).
- FT45's clip leaves the action scale, the normaliser and the controller clamp at 1.0472: the policy believes it can turn
  at 60 deg/s and is limited to 45 deg/s, as CAM-S2a-E's YC12k (clip without retraining).
- 6 blocks, df 4, 1,024 episodes per cell: resolution as above (a true null reads NEGLIGIBLE about 7 times in 10).
- The A60 cells (if reused) ran on 2026-11-24 afternoon in CAM-S2a-E, the FT cells later; the reuse rests on the binding
  and the one reproduction cell, not on a fresh run of all six.
- The legacy target's bar screening and placement queries remain TF32-exposed `cdist` paths, as in training and
  CAM-S2a-E; the densities avoid multiples of 4.
- Recorder and capture-rule limits as in CAM-S2a-E (one-step-stale detection flag in the capture rule, radial-only closing
  bound, `contact` cannot separate a bar contact from a PhysX contact with the target mesh, `target_contact_swept` also
