System and information contract

MOTAR evaluates frozen UAV tracking and close-approach policies in simulation. Its official sensor contract is RGB-D Camera + LiDAR. LiDAR supports obstacles and target-association assistance; the simulator pipeline is not a qualified live real-image perception stack.

The flat actor observation has 898 values. The network embeds it as 17 tokens, including CLS. The actor produces four components, but the task replaces its z component with PI altitude hold. Policy F uses riskcap; arc-clearance is an alternate experimental mode, not a second serial filter or a planner.

The tracker contains a six-dimensional position/velocity mean and 6 × 6 covariance. Five sampled history slots nominally span two seconds, while track expiry is five seconds. Simulator own state is not evidence of a calibrated hardware estimator.

Scene GT generates synthetic sensor observations and supplies evaluation, rewards, termination and the privileged critic. Target pose, semantic mask and visibility are not direct actor inputs. Offline real-image error estimation remains a separate lineage. Observation structure · Corrected system figure.
