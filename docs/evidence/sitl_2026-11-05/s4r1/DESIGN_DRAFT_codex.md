# S4R-1 v1 — Gazebo sensor repeatability diagnostic (draft 2026-11-10)

Preparation only; measurement NOT_RUN. Bead motar-783.11. Older motar-15g remains the dev08/replayed-sensor request and is not satisfied here.

## Existing-data dependency

S4F-1 v1 original reading and negative reproducibility finding are preserved. Fixed-grid rates remain useful; scene-matched inference is limited by 17/39 cross-run outcome agreement (chance 17.1; kappa approximately zero). Existing steps.npy plus sensor_steps.npy permit sampled trajectory, requested action, delivered frame-stamp/age and visibility reconstruction. Gzipped ULogs contain high-rate truth and control state. Raw rendered arrays, actor observations, exact receive/send timeline and target-applied frame counter were not persisted: first sensor-to-action divergence cannot be reconstructed retroactively. Existing data can therefore establish when recorded state/action summaries separate, but cannot isolate why inputs first differ.

## Fixed diagnostic question

On the unchanged S4F-1 lit_rendered/ground stack, what is the first recorded stage at which fresh boots differ, and does enabling the existing Gazebo world-contact subscriber coincide with a change beyond within-condition variation? This unit is not a comparison of policies, vehicle models, gains, sensor fields of view or floor filtering. F remains frozen. No OAK-D hardware input is required or qualified.

## Proposed fixed schedule

Use env 0 and env 1, the first two eligible G8 r2 70-bar scenes, selected by input order. For each scene use three repeats of subscriber OFF and ON. Interleave scenes by repeat; alternate condition order by scene plus repeat parity. Twelve fresh rootfs/owned boots, no retries or replacement. Same lit target, rendered floor, 0.10 m ground filter, 87 degree camera, body-fixed RGB/depth/LiDAR mount, navrl_quad_s4, existing pinned plugin/airframe and F. Record the complete source/resource/input/checkpoint manifest before launch and compare the exact intended world/spawn/target trajectory/parameter maps within scene across conditions. Preserve actual start-state discrepancies rather than resetting them away.

Gazebo seed must be an explicit fixed launch argument, recorded with argv; unlike original runs this removes an unspecified seed as one source of ambiguity. Any claim that this reproduces the old random stream is prohibited. Sensor-generated frame IDs/counters are NOT_RECORDED by the pinned plugin: record timestamp plus exact payload digest as the source-frame signature, received packet index as client packet ID and source timestamp repeat counts. Do not call packet count an actual sensor production count.

## Additive recording contract

Reuse S4F1.run_episode unchanged with owned-session, link/client and bridge/policy proxies; do not replace the loop. The contact subscriber is the only OFF/ON treatment; all new recorders are identical in both conditions. Keep raw arrivals and phase markers covering boot, initialization, takeoff/hover, control, post-terminal/landing and shutdown. Only control phase contributes first-divergence/outcome analysis. Explicit cleanup/landing exclusion is checked from phase markers.

Persist every delivered sensor payload including hover reads, packet header (sim_now, three sensor stamps, target_applied_t, target_applied_count), monotonic request/return time, latest estimated/truth/attitude/heartbeat state and raw payload signatures. Persist actual arrays to compressed per-packet files; record exact derived lidar/RGB/depth passed to perception, observation and raw actor output, governed command, target send and receipt time. Persist all MAVLink arrivals and sends with monotonic timing, source IDs/message sequence and fields, and per-type counts, COMMAND_ACK and STATUSTEXT. ULog gives accepted PX4 setpoint telemetry if the topic is present and populated; absence is NOT_RECORDED and must prevent an accepted-command causal conclusion. A sent SET_POSITION_TARGET message is not an accepted command. Contact OFF is NOT_RECORDED, not zero contacts. ON requires the floor-contact positive control plus ongoing subscriber process/coverage check. Retain full original ULogs and logged contact output.

Write canonical per-step timelines binding the chosen packet, obs, action/requested setpoint and nearest accepted setpoint/estimate/truth/contact event. State all clock mappings and resolution. Compare initial state first, then same-index control samples; report source hashes, sensor stamps/payloads, estimated/truth state, obs/action and requested/accepted control divergences separately. Report earliest resolvable disagreement and ties/unresolved ordering; do not infer causal order below recorded clock or sample resolution.

## Descriptive readings and limits

Report exact equality and max absolute differences on canonical numeric arrays, per-scene/condition pairwise terminal agreement, first differing step and simulator/wall times, start pose/velocity/attitude spread, delivered stamp/age/repeat distributions, and within-condition versus between-condition trajectory spread. No significance or equivalence gate is proposed with n=3/scene/condition. The unit can reveal a reproducibility failure and narrow its first recorded stage; it cannot prove determinism, equivalence, a causal subscriber effect or generalize to camera-only/OAK-D/real flight. Identical instrumented boots do not prove recorder noninterference with the historical uninstrumented stack.
