## What may and may not be said

- **May:** the sentences above, scoped to simulation and this condition.
- **May not:** causal wording; "a speed cap makes it worse"; "speed is not the bottleneck"; "IN_VIEW means visible";
  "BLIND dominates" (true under the nearest-point test only); any real-flight crash rate; that any latency below
  100 ms is safe; that the simulated depth model matches the OAK-D Pro.
- **For a first flight:** the simulation gives no support for a lower cap as a crash-reduction lever for this policy;
  a conservative cap chosen for test safety is a separate decision these results neither justify nor forbid.
  End-to-end latency should be measured and bounded on the vehicle, since the simulated crash rates assume zero.
